Head-to-head challenge payout split

This controls how the pot from every challenge is split once a winner is confirmed. The two numbers must add up to 100.

Raffle countdown

Default countdown duration (in days) for every new raffle account. The public site shows this countdown on each raffle card so buyers know how long is left. You can override this on a per-account basis from the Raffle Accounts page.
